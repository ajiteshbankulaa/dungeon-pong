The new original two-movement composition, arrangements, voicings and mix are authored for What Returns. Both movements use the credited Salamander A3 sample, resampled, filtered and layered. The authoring source is tools/build_score.py. what-returns-suite.mp3 combines both movements, deliberate silence, and the final chord for listening outside the game.

Morning is an original sparse arrangement using the same credited Salamander A3 piano source. Its editable authoring script is tools/build_morning.py.

The current v3 three-movement score is authored in tools/build_soundtrack.py using the same credited piano sample. Original arrangements and mixes differ across the three acts. No generated continuous noise bed is played.
